## Key defined terms

- **Additional License Terms** (Section 1.15): this License together
  with supplemental terms — like an addendum or rider — that a
  Contributor attaches to their own modifications or a Derivative Work.
  These add to this License; they don't replace it.
- **Different License Terms** (Section 1.16): anything that is not
  governed by this License or by Additional License Terms — i.e., terms
  a Contributor uses in place of this License for their own
  modifications, as permitted under Section 5.

## How to apply the license to your Work

Copy the notice block from the Appendix at the end of the license file,
fill in the bracketed fields, and attach it to your Work as described
there. Including a URL for the license text in that notice is
**voluntary** — you may delete the `[URL]` line if you don't have one to
point to.
